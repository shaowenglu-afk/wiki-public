## **Key Findings**
> **主要发现**

*   Australia is among the leading adopters of Claude, accounting for 1.6% of global Claude.ai traffic. Per capita, Australians' use of Claude is more than four times higher than expected for the size of its population.｜澳大利亚是Claude的主要采用国之一，占全球Claude.ai流量的1.6%。按人均计算，澳大利亚人使用Claude的频率是根据其人口规模预期值的四倍以上。
*   Adoption within Australia is concentrated in two states: New South Wales (37% of conversations) and Victoria (31%). Per capita Claude usage is lower in every other state and territory.｜澳大利亚境内的采用集中在两个州：新南威尔士州（占对话的37%）和维多利亚州（31%）。其他每个州和领地的人均Claude使用量均较低。
*   Australia's mix of use-cases generally tracks with its Anglosphere peers: 46% of Claude conversations are for work, 7% for coursework, 47% for personal use. This is a profile typical of high-income, high-adoption economies.｜澳大利亚的用例组合总体上与其英语国家同伴相似：46%的Claude对话用于工作，7%用于课业，47%用于个人用途。这是高收入、高采用率经济体的典型特征。
*   But Australia's mix of tasks is more diverse. Like other countries, Computer & Mathematical tasks remain the single largest category in our dataset—but this category is about 8 percentage points below the global baseline. It's offset by higher-than-average volumes of office, sales, management, and personal life tasks.｜但澳大利亚的任务组合更加多样。与其他国家一样，计算机与数学任务仍是我们数据集中最大的单一类别——但该类别比全球基准低约8个百分点。偏低的占比被高于平均水平的办公室、销售、管理和个人生活任务所抵消。
*   Users in Australia tend to prompt Claude for more complex tasks, as measured by the estimated years of schooling required to understand the prompt. At the same time, we estimate that the length of time these tasks would've taken _without_ AI is roughly 20% less than average. In other words, relative to how the rest of the world uses Claude, Australians' tasks are associated with a higher education level but a shorter duration.｜澳大利亚用户倾向于向Claude提出更复杂的任务，以理解提示所需的预估受教育年限衡量。与此同时，我们估计这些任务在没有AI的情况下所需的时间比平均水平短约20%。换言之，相对于世界其他地区使用Claude的方式，澳大利亚人的任务与更高的教育水平但更短的持续时间相关联。
*   Like many other high-adoption economies, Australia registers as having a relatively low "AI autonomy" score (3.38 on a 1–5 scale), which suggests that Australians use Claude in more collaborative, less delegated ways.｜与许多其他高采用率经济体一样，澳大利亚的"AI自主性"得分相对较低（在1-5分制中为3.38分），这表明澳大利亚人以更协作、更少委托的方式使用Claude。

## **High adoption overall, unevenly distributed across states and territories**
> **整体采用率高，但在各州和领地间分布不均**

Australia accounts for 1.6% of global Claude.ai traffic, ranking eleventh among all countries in our February 2026 sample (Figure 1). Its Anthropic AI Usage Index (AUI) is 4.1, which means Australians use Claude over four times more than its working-age population would predict. This places Australia among the highest per capita adopters of Claude with ranking seventh behind Singapore, Israel, Luxembourg, Switzerland, the United States, and Canada.
澳大利亚占全球Claude.ai流量的1.6%，在我们2026年2月样本的所有国家中排名第十一（图1）。其Anthropic AI使用指数（AUI）为4.1，意味着澳大利亚人使用Claude的频率是其劳动年龄人口预期值的四倍以上。这使澳大利亚成为Claude人均采用率最高的国家之一，排名第七，位于新加坡、以色列、卢森堡、瑞士、美国和加拿大之后。

Within Australia, usage is concentrated in the most populous states. New South Wales accounts for 37.2% of conversations, followed by Victoria at 30.8%, Queensland at 17.7%, and the remaining states and territories at a combined 14% (Figure 2). States and territories' working age population size explains much of this ordering. However, adjusting for this, New South Wales has an AUI of 1.20 and Victoria an AUI of 1.19. These are the only two states with higher than expected per capita adoption; every other state or territory has an AUI below 1, with adoption lowest in Western Australia at 0.68, Tasmania at 0.32, and the Northern Territory at 0.12.
在澳大利亚内部，使用量集中在人口最多的州。新南威尔士州占对话量的37.2%，其次是维多利亚州（30.8%）、昆士兰州（17.7%），其余州和领地合计占14%（图2）。各州和领地的劳动年龄人口规模在很大程度上解释了这一排序。然而，经过调整后，新南威尔士州的AUI为1.20，维多利亚州为1.19。这两个州是唯一人均采用率高于预期的州；其他每个州或领地的AUI均低于1，采用率最低的是西澳大利亚州（0.68）、塔斯马尼亚州（0.32）和北领地（0.12）。

In contrast to the strong association between income and Claude usage per capita we [observe across countries](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-september-2025-report), income does not appear to predict adoption across Australian states and territories (Figure 3). That said, since we can only compare across the eight, the lack of correlation between income and usage is suggestive rather than conclusive.
与我们在各国之间观察到的收入与人均Claude使用量之间的强关联相比，收入似乎并不能预测澳大利亚各州和领地的采用情况（图3）。尽管如此，由于我们只能在这八个州和领地之间进行比较，收入与使用量之间缺乏相关性更多是提示性的，而非确定性的。

Local workforce composition is a likely factor for why adoption appears decoupled from income across Australian states and territories: mining-heavy Western Australia, the state with the highest GSP per capita, has low usage per capita. The Northern Territory likewise has high GSP per capita and low AUI. This likely reflects these sparsely populated states and territories having a relatively smaller share of workers in occupations where Claude tends to be adopted. The Australian Capital Territory has above average income but lower-than-expected Claude usage per capita, which may reflect barriers to adoption among its large public sector workforce. The two states with the greatest adoption, New South Wales and Victoria, have slightly below average income; high adoption in these states likely reflects the higher share of workers in finance, professional services, and tech sectors, where Claude usage tends to be higher.
当地劳动力构成可能是澳大利亚各州和领地采用率与收入脱钩的一个主要因素：以采矿业为主的西澳大利亚州是人均州内生产总值最高的州，但人均使用量较低。北领地同样拥有较高的人均州内生产总值和较低的AUI。这可能反映了这些人口稀少的州和领地在Claude倾向于被采用的职业中拥有相对较小份额的劳动者。澳大利亚首都领地收入高于平均水平，但人均Claude使用量低于预期，这可能反映了其庞大公共部门劳动力采用Claude存在的障碍。采用率最高的两个州——新南威尔士州和维多利亚州——收入略低于平均水平；这两个州的高采用率可能反映了金融、专业服务和科技行业劳动者的较高比例，而这些行业的Claude使用率往往更高。

## **Australia's use of Claude resembles its Anglosphere peers**
> **澳大利亚的Claude使用方式与其英语国家同伴相似**

Our fourth [Economic Index report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report) introduced four economic primitives: the use-case mix, the degree of autonomy afforded to Claude, task success, and task complexity. Figure 4 shows where Australia falls on each of these relative to other Anglosphere countries (in orange), other high-adoption economies (in grey), and all countries in our sample (in purple).
我们的第四份经济指数报告引入了四个经济基元：用例组合、赋予Claude的自主程度、任务成功率和任务复杂度。图4显示了澳大利亚在这些指标上相对于其他英语国家（橙色）、其他高采用率经济体（灰色）和我们样本中所有国家（紫色）的位置。

In terms of how Claude is used, Australia is similar to its Anglosphere peers. 46% of Australian conversations are classified as work-related, close to the median and in the middle of the Anglosphere range. Coursework accounts for 7% of Australian use, which is toward the bottom of the distribution (and below the US, UK, and Canada); countries with lower per capita adoption show coursework shares two to three times as high. The share of conversations that are for personal use, at 47%, is near the top of the distribution.
在Claude的使用方式上，澳大利亚与其英语国家同伴相似。46%的澳大利亚对话被归类为与工作相关，接近中位数，处于英语国家范围的中间位置。课业占澳大利亚使用量的7%，处于分布的底部（低于美国、英国和加拿大）；人均采用率较低的国家课业占比高出两到三倍。个人用途的对话占比47%，接近分布的顶部。

This pattern for Australia is consistent with the relationship we documented in our economic primitives [report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report) between per capita income and use cases: richer countries tend to have lower coursework shares and higher personal use shares.
澳大利亚的这一模式与我们在经济基元报告中记录的人均收入与用例之间的关系一致：较富裕的国家往往具有较低的课业占比和较高的个人用途占比。

Australia registers toward the low end of AI autonomy, at 3.38 on a 1–5 scale, with other Anglosphere countries close by. Lower autonomy indicates that users retain more decision-making control in their conversations, rather than delegating fully to the model. This is consistent with high-adoption economies tending to use Claude in more collaborative, less directive ways.
澳大利亚的AI自主性处于较低水平，在1-5分制中为3.38分，其他英语国家也接近这一水平。较低的自主性表明用户在对话中保留了更多的决策控制权，而非完全委托给模型。这与高采用率经济体倾向于以更协作、更少指令性的方式使用Claude的趋势一致。

Australia sits below the median on one measure of task complexity, but toward the top on another. Australia's average task itself is relatively less time-intensive: it would take a skilled professional an estimated 2.7 hours to complete the task without AI, compared to the cross-country average of 3.3 hours. But prompts from Australian users are estimated to require 11.9 years of schooling to understand, which is comparable to other Anglosphere countries and above the global median. Both patterns are consistent with the cross-country relationships we documented in our [January Economic Index report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report), where the intensity of Claude adoption correlates positively with human prompt sophistication and negatively with estimates of the "no-AI" task duration.
澳大利亚在一项任务复杂度指标上低于中位数，但在另一项指标上接近顶部。澳大利亚的平均任务本身相对不那么耗时：一位熟练的专业人员在没有AI的情况下完成该任务估计需要2.7小时，而跨国平均水平为3.3小时。但澳大利亚用户的提示估计需要11.9年的受教育年限才能理解，这与其他英语国家相当，高于全球中位数。这两种模式都与我们在1月经济指数报告中记录的跨国关系一致：Claude采用强度与人类提示的复杂程度正相关，与"无AI"任务持续时间估计负相关。

Australia's Claude use is more diverse than usage among peer Anglosphere countries and relative to the broader global average. Figure 5 shows the share of each geography's usage that is covered by its 100 most common tasks (where tasks are classified according to O*NET, a taxonomy of tasks that characterize jobs throughout the economy). Lower values on this figure indicate that Claude is used for a wider range of tasks, since the top 100 occupy a smaller share. Australia's top 100 tasks account for 47.3% of its usage, below the US (47.7%), the UK (48.3%), and Canada (50.2%), and five percentage points below the global figure of 52.3%.
澳大利亚的Claude使用比英语国家同伴以及更广泛的全球平均水平更加多元。图5显示了每个地区使用量中最常见的100项任务所覆盖的比例（任务按O*NET分类，这是一套描述整个经济体职业特征的任务分类体系）。该图上的较低值表明Claude被用于更广泛的任务范围，因为前100项任务占据的份额更小。澳大利亚前100项任务占其使用量的47.3%，低于美国（47.7%）、英国（48.3%）和加拿大（50.2%），比全球数字52.3%低五个百分点。

The broader diversity of usage within Australia comes mostly from a lower share of coding-related tasks. Figure 6 shows how the composition of Australia's top 100 tasks differs from the global top 100 by SOC major group (broad occupation categories defined by the US Bureau of Labor Statistics). Computer and Mathematical tasks are 8.0 percentage points lower in Australia. No other category of use approaches that magnitude of difference to the global average, though education-related tasks are nearly three percentage points below global parity. The offsetting positives are spread across many groups, led by Management (+2.3pp), Office and Administrative Support (+1.3pp) and Life, Physical, and Social Science occupations (+1.3pp).
澳大利亚内部更广泛的使用多样性主要来自编码相关任务占比较低。图6显示了澳大利亚前100项任务的构成按SOC主要职业群（美国劳工统计局定义的广泛职业类别）与全球前100项任务的差异。计算机与数学任务在澳大利亚低8.0个百分点。其他使用类别与全球平均水平的差距均未接近这一幅度，尽管教育相关任务比全球平均水平低近三个百分点。抵消这一差距的正向因素分布在许多群体中，其中管理类（+2.3个百分点）、办公室与行政支持（+1.3个百分点）和生命、物理及社会科学职业（+1.3个百分点）领先。

At the level of individual request clusters—categories of what users ask Claude to do—the pattern is similar (Figure 7). The cluster that is most underrepresented in Australia relative to the global distribution is general coding assistance, at 13.5% of Australian use compared to 16.8% globally. Document translation is also underrepresented, consistent with Australia being a predominantly English-speaking market. The most overrepresented clusters are a mix of personal use (such as personal life management (+1.9pp) and health and well-being support (+1.8pp)), and non-technical professional work, including workplace correspondence (+1.7pp), business documents (+1.6pp), and financial guidance (+1.3pp).
在单个请求集群层面——用户要求Claude执行的任务类别——模式相似（图7）。相对于全球分布，在澳大利亚最为欠代表的集群是通用编码辅助，占澳大利亚使用量的13.5%，而全球为16.8%。文档翻译也处于欠代表状态，这与澳大利亚是一个以英语为主的市场一致。最过度代表的集群混合了个人用途（如个人生活管理（+1.9个百分点）和健康与福祉支持（+1.8个百分点））以及非技术性专业工作，包括职场通信（+1.7个百分点）、商业文件（+1.6个百分点）和财务指导（+1.3个百分点）。

Higher-adoption countries generally use Claude more broadly, so part of Australia's lower coding share is expected. Australia's Computer and Mathematical shortfall of 8.0pp sits right in line with the Anglosphere average of 8.9pp. Where Australia diverges from its peers is in what fills the gap: it leans further into Management (+2.3pp vs the Anglosphere's +1.2pp) and Office and Administrative Support (+1.3pp vs +0.1pp), rather than Educational Instruction (−2.7pp vs +1.6pp for peers).
采用率较高的国家通常使用Claude的范围更广，因此澳大利亚编码占比较低的部分原因在预期之内。澳大利亚计算机与数学类别8.0个百分点的不足与英语国家平均水平8.9个百分点基本吻合。澳大利亚与同伴不同之处在于填补这一差距的内容：它更倾向于管理类（+2.3个百分点，而英语国家为+1.2个百分点）和办公室与行政支持（+1.3个百分点，而英语国家为+0.1个百分点），而非教育教学（−2.7个百分点，而同伴为+1.6个百分点）。

## **Conclusion**
> **结论**

Australia is among the leading per capita adopters of Claude, with usage more than four times what its working-age population would predict. The character of that usage—predominantly work and personal rather than coursework, sophisticated prompts addressing relatively short tasks, and with less delegation to Claude—closely tracks other Anglosphere economies and cross-country relationships evident within the Anthropic Economic Index.
澳大利亚是Claude人均采用率最高的国家之一，使用量超过其劳动年龄人口预期值的四倍以上。这种使用特征——主要用于工作和个人用途而非课业、复杂提示针对相对较短的任务、对Claude的委托程度较低——与其他英语国家经济体以及Anthropic经济指数中明显的跨国关系密切吻合。

Where Australia is more distinctive is in the breadth of what Claude is used for. Australian usage is more diverse than the global aggregate, almost entirely because of a lower share of coding-related work; offsetting gains are spread across office, sales, management, and personal life categories rather than concentrated in any one category. As with other high adoption countries, users in Australia tend to collaborate more with Claude and delegate decision-making less often.
澳大利亚更具独特性的地方在于Claude用途的广度。澳大利亚的使用比全球总体更加多样，几乎完全是因为编码相关工作的占比较低；抵消性的增长分散在办公室、销售、管理和个人生活类别中，而非集中在任何一个类别。与其他高采用率国家一样，澳大利亚用户倾向于更多地与Claude协作，较少委托决策。

Within Australia, adoption is concentrated in New South Wales and Victoria, and the state-level variation tracks workforce composition rather than income per capita—consistent with the pattern across US states but in contrast to the income gradient across countries.
在澳大利亚内部，采用集中在新南威尔士州和维多利亚州，州级差异追踪劳动力构成而非人均收入——这与美国各州的模式一致，但与各国之间的收入梯度形成对比。
