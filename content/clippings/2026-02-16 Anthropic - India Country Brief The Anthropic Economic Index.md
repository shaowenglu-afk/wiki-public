## Executive summary
> 执行摘要

India, already the world's largest exporter of IT services, is home to one of the world's fastest-growing AI user bases. Understanding how AI is being used in India—and how it differs from other countries—is essential for informing AI policy, investment, and deployment in the country. This brief provides insights on Claude.ai use in India, drawing on data from the [fourth Anthropic Economic Index report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report) covering ~1 million Claude.ai conversations globally during November 2025. India accounts for 5.8% of total Claude.ai use, second only to the United States. Yet current adoption remains concentrated, pointing to significant opportunities to expand access more broadly across the population.
印度已是全球最大的 IT 服务出口国，同时也拥有全球增速最快的 AI 用户群体之一。了解 AI 在印度的使用方式——以及它与其他国家的差异——对于制定印度的 AI 政策、投资和部署决策至关重要。本简报基于[Anthropic 经济指数第四期报告](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)的数据，提供 Claude.ai 在印度使用情况的洞察，该报告覆盖 2025 年 11 月全球约 100 万条 Claude.ai 对话。印度占全球 Claude.ai 使用总量的 5.8%，仅次于美国。然而，当前的采用率仍较为集中，这也指向了在更大范围人群中扩展 AI 普及的重大机遇。

The findings point to a user base that applies AI more heavily in professional contexts, delegates more autonomy to it, and brings Claude tasks that are substantially more time-consuming to complete without assistance. Higher shares of complex tasks that humans could not complete alone suggest that Indian users are using the technology at the frontier.
研究结果表明，印度用户群体更多地将 AI 应用于专业场景，向 AI 委托更多自主权，并向 Claude 提交若无 AI 协助将耗费大量时间的任务。人类独立完成的复杂任务占比更高，表明印度用户正在前沿层面使用这一技术。

## India is among the leading countries in global AI adoption
> 印度是全球 AI 采用的领先国家之一

India ranks second among all countries by share of total Claude.ai use, trailing only the United States. However, on a per-capita basis, adjusting for the working-age population, India ranks 101st out of 116 countries with sufficient observation volume, below other countries in Asia such as Singapore or Malaysia. This gap suggests that India's high Claude use overall reflects the sheer size of its population, not that the average person is using Claude heavily. This points to significant opportunities to increase adoption.
按 Claude.ai 使用总量占比排名，印度在所有国家中位居第二，仅次于美国。然而，按劳动年龄人口的人均使用量计算，印度在观察量充足的 116 个国家中排名第 101 位，低于新加坡或马来西亚等其他亚洲国家。这一差距表明，印度整体 Claude 使用量高，反映的是其庞大的人口基数，而非人均用量水平高。这也指向了大幅提升采用率的重要机遇。

## Concentrated use within India
> 印度国内的使用集中度

### Geographic concentration
> 地理集中度

Use is concentrated in a small number of highly economically active states. Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Delhi together account for over half of India's total Claude.ai use. This pattern closely mirrors India's IT sector geography and urban economic output.
使用量集中在少数经济高度活跃的邦。马哈拉施特拉邦、泰米尔纳德邦、卡纳塔克邦和德里合计占印度 Claude.ai 使用总量的一半以上。这一格局与印度 IT 行业的地理分布和城市经济产出高度吻合。

这四个邦集中了班加罗尔、海德拉巴、金奈、孟买和德里 NCR，表明当前 AI 采用主要由印度既有的技术劳动力群体驱动，而非广泛的消费者普及。

## Economic primitives: how India uses AI differently
> 经济基元：印度如何差异化使用 AI

[Our latest Economic Index report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report) introduces "economic primitives"—fundamental measurements of how humans and AI collaborate. Comparing India to the global average reveals several distinctive patterns.
[我们最新的经济指数报告](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)引入了"经济基元"——衡量人类与 AI 协作方式的基础性指标。将印度与全球平均水平进行比较，揭示了几个独特模式。

![Image 5](https://www.anthropic.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww-cdn.anthropic.com%2Fimages%2F4zrzovbb%2Fwebsite%2F769426797ecc9b3fd234277eafc36aa248169cb6-3040x1920.png&w=3840&q=75)

**Figure 4.** Comparing India's Claude.ai use to the global average. The panels compare India (N = 58,098) against the global average (N = 975,160) across nine economic primitives. November 2025 data.
**图 4.** 印度 Claude.ai 使用情况与全球平均水平的比较。各面板在九个经济基元上对印度（N = 58,098）与全球平均水平（N = 975,160）进行比较。数据来自 2025 年 11 月。

**Greater productivity speedup.** Indian users take on average 14.8 minutes to complete tasks with AI that would take 3.8 hours without AI—a 15x speedup. Globally, users take on average 15.4 minutes to complete tasks that would take 3.1 hours without AI—a 12x speedup. This suggests that AI is delivering outsized productivity gains on the more complex tasks Indian users bring to it.
**更高的生产力加速。** 印度用户借助 AI 完成任务平均需要 14.8 分钟，而若无 AI 则需 3.8 小时——加速倍数为 15 倍。全球用户借助 AI 完成任务平均需要 15.4 分钟，而若无 AI 则需 3.1 小时——加速倍数为 12 倍。这表明 AI 在印度用户带来的更复杂任务上实现了超额的生产力提升。

**Stronger work orientation.** 51.3% of Indian Claude.ai use is work-related, compared to 46% globally. Coursework accounts for 20.9% (vs. 19.3% globally) and personal use for 27.8% (vs. 34.7% globally). The work-heavy, lower-personal-use profile is consistent with India's large professional services sector and the finding from the main report that lower-GDP-per-capita countries tend towards work and coursework over personal use.
**更强的工作导向。** 印度 Claude.ai 使用量的 51.3% 与工作相关，全球为 46%。课程作业占 20.9%（全球为 19.3%），个人使用占 27.8%（全球为 34.7%）。工作占比高、个人使用占比低的特征与印度庞大的专业服务业相符，也与主报告的发现一致——人均 GDP 较低的国家倾向于将 AI 用于工作和课程作业而非个人用途。

**Higher AI autonomy.** Indian users delegate more decision-making autonomy to AI (3.60 vs. 3.38 globally on a 1–5 scale, where 1 means no delegation and 5 means extreme delegation). This suggests greater willingness to let AI operate independently rather than using it purely as an assistant.
**更高的 AI 自主度。** 印度用户向 AI 委托更多决策自主权（在 1–5 分量表上为 3.60，全球为 3.38，1 表示完全不委托，5 表示极度委托）。这表明印度用户更愿意让 AI 独立运作，而非仅将其作为辅助工具。

**Lower human-only ability.** One of the data points we measure is whether AI is being used to do something a human couldn't do on their own, like writing code in a language they don't know. We find that 84.6% of tasks could be completed by a human alone (vs. 87.9% globally), suggesting Indian users more frequently bring tasks to AI that they could not easily accomplish independently.
**更低的人类独立完成能力。** 我们衡量的数据点之一是 AI 是否被用于完成人类无法独立完成的事情，例如用自己不懂的语言编写代码。我们发现 84.6% 的任务可由人类独立完成（全球为 87.9%），表明印度用户更频繁地将无法轻易独立完成的任务带给 AI。

**Prompting skills matter.** As a proxy for the skills humans and AI bring to the conversation, we estimate the years of education someone would need to understand the user prompt or the AI response in a conversation. We find that the human education level of prompts (12.2 years) and AI education level of responses (12.5 years) are relatively similar, mirroring a global pattern where input quality shapes output quality. Comparing country averages for AI education, India ranks in the top 10%, indicating Indian users are getting sophisticated outputs from Claude.
**提示词技巧很重要。** 作为衡量人类和 AI 各自带入对话的技能水平的替代指标，我们估算了理解对话中用户提示词或 AI 回应所需的受教育年限。我们发现提示词的人类教育水平（12.2 年）与回应的 AI 教育水平（12.5 年）相对接近，印证了全球范围内输入质量决定输出质量的规律。比较各国 AI 教育水平均值，印度排名前 10%，表明印度用户正从 Claude 获得高质量的输出。

## Implications
> 政策含义

**Broadening AI's economic impact will require looking beyond software and IT services.** 45.2% of tasks map to software-related occupations—the highest share of any country. Four states (Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Delhi) account for over half of all use. This mirrors the geography of India's IT sector and suggests that current AI adoption is largely an extension of existing professional strengths and workflows focused on IT.
**扩大 AI 的经济影响需要超越软件和 IT 服务行业。** 45.2% 的任务映射到软件相关职业——在所有国家中占比最高。四个邦（马哈拉施特拉邦、泰米尔纳德邦、卡纳塔克邦和德里）占全部使用量的一半以上。这与印度 IT 行业的地理分布相吻合，表明当前 AI 采用在很大程度上是对既有专业优势和 IT 导向工作流的延伸。

**Investing in AI can provide substantial and measurable productivity gains.** Indian users apply AI to tasks that would otherwise take 3.8 hours, compressing them to ~15 minutes—a 15x speedup, compared to 12x globally. This means that India is already extracting significant value from AI: bringing harder tasks and compressing the time needed to complete these tasks further than the global average.
**投资 AI 能够带来可观且可量化的生产力提升。** 印度用户将原本需要 3.8 小时的任务交给 AI 处理，压缩至约 15 分钟——15 倍的加速，而全球为 12 倍。这意味着印度已经从 AI 中提取了可观价值：处理更难的任务，并比全球平均水平更大程度地压缩完成这些任务所需的时间。

**Closing the gap between absolute and per-capita use requires addressing structural barriers.** India ranks 2nd in total use but 101st in per-capita use. The gap between these two figures reflects both India's large population and how narrowly concentrated current adoption is. Globally, per-capita AI adoption is strongly correlated with per-capita income. India's per-capita use is consistent with what this relationship would predict. Without addressing structural barriers related to income, digital infrastructure, and awareness outside the IT sector, Indian AI adoption is likely to remain concentrated.
**缩小绝对使用量与人均使用量之间的差距需要消除结构性障碍。** 印度总使用量排名第 2，但人均使用量排名第 101。这两个数字之间的差距既反映了印度庞大的人口基数，也反映了当前采用的高度集中性。全球来看，人均 AI 采用与人均收入高度相关，印度的人均使用量与这一关系的预测值一致。若不解决与收入、数字基础设施以及 IT 行业之外的认知度相关的结构性障碍，印度的 AI 采用可能将持续集中。

**Embracing AI autonomy appears to be serving Indian users well.** Higher autonomy scores, longer baseline task times, and frequent use for tasks humans could do alone suggest that Indian professionals are trusting AI to make decisions and using it to enhance human capabilities.
**拥抱 AI 自主性似乎正在为印度用户带来良好效果。** 更高的自主度评分、更长的基准任务时长，以及频繁将人类本可独立完成的任务交给 AI 处理，表明印度专业人士正在信任 AI 做出决策，并利用它来增强人类能力。

**Investing in AI skills could have high returns.** The strong correlation between prompt sophistication and response quality in the global data suggests that training programs focused on effective AI use—particularly for workers outside India's current IT-heavy user base—could meaningfully improve the returns from wider AI adoption.
**投资 AI 技能可能带来高回报。** 全球数据中提示词复杂性与回应质量之间的强相关性表明，专注于有效 AI 使用的培训项目——尤其针对印度当前 IT 密集型用户群体之外的从业者——可能大幅提升更广泛 AI 采用的回报。

### Methodology
> 方法论

This analysis draws on privacy-preserving data from Claude.ai consumer use from November 13–20, 2025, as described in the [fourth Anthropic Economic Index report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report). Economic primitives are computed using the methodology detailed in that report. Geographic assignment uses IP-based geolocation. Occupation and task classification are based on mappings to the O*NET task taxonomy and SOC occupation groups. For country-level rankings, we only include countries with at least 200 observations in our sample because of the uncertainty of the measure for low-usage countries in our random sample. The underlying data includes Claude.ai Free, Pro, and Max usage.
本分析基于 2025 年 11 月 13 日至 20 日 Claude.ai 消费者使用的隐私保护数据，具体方法详见[Anthropic 经济指数第四期报告](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)。经济基元使用该报告中详述的方法计算。地理分配使用基于 IP 的地理定位。职业和任务分类基于 O*NET 任务分类体系和 SOC 职业群组的映射。对于国家级排名，由于随机样本中低使用量国家的测量不确定性，我们仅纳入样本中观测数至少为 200 的国家。底层数据包括 Claude.ai 免费版、专业版和 Max 版的使用情况。

_For the full methodology, global findings, and time-series analysis, see the [Anthropic Economic Index January 2026 report](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)._
_完整方法论、全球发现及时间序列分析，请参阅 [Anthropic 经济指数 2026 年 1 月报告](https://www.anthropic.com/research/anthropic-economic-index-january-2026-report)。_
